# CurbSense Adaptation and Attribution

CurbSense adapts selected reproducibility and harness patterns from the MIT-licensed `BRYANN2K/llm-engineering-benchmark` by Bryan N.: frozen task manifests, task/prompt/model/grader/environment identity hashes, runner boundaries, timeout/error separation, validation, golden tests, and report aggregation.

Removed or replaced: coding-agent tasks, starter repositories, patch evaluation, Hermes assumptions, software-engineering graders, private-repository commitments, unrelated route/pricing logic, and historical benchmark reports.

Original CurbSense work: eight synthetic curb/parking packets, structured evidence and policy fields, deterministic SVG camera assets, independent decision derivation, safety/abstention/temporal/permit/payment/zone/contradiction rules, calibration metrics, adapters, tests, and submission documentation.

AI tools assisted with implementation and drafting. The author is responsible for reviewing every task, policy, reference, grader rule, transcript, and reported result before submission.
